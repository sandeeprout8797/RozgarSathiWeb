Our Story — From Personal Tragedy to Purpose-Driven Impact

RozgarSathi was founded by Sandeep Rout , along with co-founder Vaibhav Singh , inspired by a deeply personal experience. Sandeep’s elder brother, an engineering graduate from a reputed government college, faced a sudden denial of campus placement assistance at the last moment — despite years of hard work and family investment in his education.

This unexpected setback led to prolonged struggle and emotional distress for his brother, and financial hardship for the family. Witnessing this, Sandeep resolved not to wait for change from outside. He chose to act — founding RozgarSathi as the only “sathi” you need for your next job, committed to helping candidates find real opportunities and career stability.

Why Government Efforts Alone Have Not Solved the Unemployment Crisis

India continues to face systemic challenges in reducing unemployment and providing quality employment opportunities for its growing workforce, especially youth. Even with multiple government schemes, several underlying issues persist that contribute to the problem of unemployment in India and indicate areas where government job creation shortcomings remain significant.

Jobless Growth in India: India’s economic growth has often not translated into proportional job creation, especially in labour-intensive sectors. Even with economic expansion, many sectors still lack enough formal employment opportunities.
Skill Mismatch and Unemployment: A major barrier to employment is the gap between the skills imparted by conventional education and those demanded by industry, leaving many graduates underemployed.
Insufficient Job Creation Schemes: Government skilling programs often do not translate into lasting employment at scale, with only a fraction of trainees securing jobs after training.
Education-Industry Disconnect: Outdated curriculum and weak industry partnerships leave many candidates ill-prepared for modern job demands.
Informality and Underemployment: A significant portion of the workforce remains in informal roles lacking stability and benefits, contributing to continued underemployment.

These factors explain why unemployment is rising in India despite government efforts. RozgarSathi directly addresses these gaps with personalized career guidance, industry-aligned preparation, and effective employment pathways.
