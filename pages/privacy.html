Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At RozgarSathi , we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of job seekers, employers, and visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our job recruitment platform.

1. Information We Collect

a) Personal Information (Job Seekers)

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location / address
  • Date of birth (if provided)
  • Resume / CV and profile details

b) Professional Information

  • Education details
  • Work experience
  • Skills and certifications
  • Job preferences and salary expectations

c) Employer Information

  • Company name
  • Business email address
  • Contact person details
  • Job postings and hiring requirements

d) Technical Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and usage data
  • Cookies and tracking technologies

2. How We Collect Information

We collect information through:

  • User registration and profile creation
  • Job applications and resume uploads
  • Employer job posting forms
  • Contact forms and email communications
  • Cookies and analytics tools

3. Purpose of Data Collection

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Match candidates with suitable job opportunities
  • Allow employers to view candidate profiles
  • Send job alerts, updates, and notifications
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorized access

4. Resume & Profile Visibility

Candidate resumes and profiles are visible only to registered employers and recruiters. Users can edit, update, or delete their profiles at any time through their account dashboard.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share your information with:

  • Employers and recruiters for recruitment purposes
  • Trusted service providers (hosting, email, analytics)
  • Legal authorities if required by law

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Cookies Policy

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience, analyze traffic, and remember login sessions. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some features may not function properly.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable security practices including secure servers, restricted access, and technical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

8. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review your personal data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and personal data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

9. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill recruitment purposes or comply with legal obligations. Users may request data deletion at any time.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such external sites.

11. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

12. Legal Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: privaterozgarsathi@gmail.com
Website: https://rozgarsathi.co.in/

By using our website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.
