Privacy Policy, Disclaimer & Refund Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Privacy Policy

At RozgarSathi, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of job seekers, employers, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our recruitment and employment services.

1.1 Information We Collect

a) Personal Information (Job Seekers)

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location / address
  • Date of birth (if provided)
  • Resume / CV and profile details

b) Professional Information

  • Education details
  • Work experience
  • Skills and certifications
  • Job preferences and salary expectations

c) Employer Information

  • Company name
  • Business email address
  • Contact person details
  • Job postings and hiring requirements

d) Technical Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and usage data
  • Cookies and tracking technologies

1.2 How We Collect Information

  • User registration and profile creation
  • Job applications and resume uploads
  • Employer job posting forms
  • Contact forms and email communications
  • Cookies and analytics tools

1.3 Purpose of Data Collection

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Match candidates with suitable job opportunities
  • Allow employers to view candidate profiles
  • Send job alerts, updates, and notifications
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorized access

1.4 Resume & Profile Visibility

Candidate resumes and profiles are visible only to registered employers and recruiters. Users may edit, update, or delete their profiles at any time through their account dashboard.

1.5 Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Data may be shared only with employers, recruiters, trusted service providers, or legal authorities when required by law.

1.6 Cookies Policy

We use cookies to enhance user experience, analyze traffic, and manage login sessions. Disabling cookies may affect certain site features.

1.7 Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures including secure servers, restricted access, and technical safeguards. However, no internet transmission is completely secure.

1.8 User Rights

  • Access and review personal data
  • Update or correct information
  • Delete account and personal data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

1.9 Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as required for recruitment services or legal compliance. Users may request deletion at any time.

1.10 Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age.

1.11 Legal Compliance

This policy complies with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

2. Disclaimer

RozgarSathi is a recruitment facilitation platform. We do not guarantee job placement, salary, job role, job location, or employment duration.

Final hiring decisions are solely between the employer and the candidate. RozgarSathi is not responsible for employer conduct, job terms, or post-placement disputes.

Users are advised to independently verify job offers before accepting any employment.

3. Refund Policy

All payments made towards registration, consultation, training, or recruitment services are subject to the following terms:

  • All service fees are non-refundable
  • No refund once a service has started
  • No refund if a candidate rejects offered opportunities
  • Delays caused by employer response are not refundable

Any exceptional refund request will be reviewed solely at the discretion of RozgarSathi management.

4. Policy Updates

RozgarSathi reserves the right to modify these policies at any time. Changes become effective immediately upon posting.
